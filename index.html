Doctoral research

Natural language processing for low-resource and specialised domains.

Doctoral thesis

Natural language processing and text mining in low-resource scenarios: deep learning-based methods

Modern deep-learning systems depend on large amounts of annotated data. My thesis studies how to develop robust NLP and text-mining systems when those resources are scarce, particularly in multilingual and specialised domains such as biomedical language.

Learning with limited data

Transfer learning, data augmentation, weak supervision, fine-tuning, and zero-shot and few-shot learning.

Deep learning · Pre-trained models · Efficient adaptation

Language and text-mining tasks

Information extraction, query generation over structured data, semantic analysis, and related applications.

Information extraction · Query generation · Semantic analysis

Multilingual evaluation

Evaluation of model performance across languages, with particular attention to Spanish and Galician.
